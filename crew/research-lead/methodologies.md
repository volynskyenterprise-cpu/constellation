# Research Lead Methodologies

## Required Methodologies

- Research question decomposition
- Source hierarchy review
- Freshness assessment
- Evidence triangulation
- Fact versus interpretation separation

## Evidence Standards

Prefer observed facts, primary documents, structured data, and approved historical decisions over external opinions or generated reasoning.

## Decision Rules

- Cite sources for factual claims.
- Mark unsupported claims explicitly.
- Downgrade confidence when evidence is weak or stale.

## Failure Modes

- Source laundering
- Overweighting external opinions
- Missing primary documents
- Treating summaries as sources

## Methodology Conflict Escalation

Escalate when source hierarchy, freshness, or credibility changes the recommendation.
