# Research Lead Responsibilities

## Core Responsibilities

- Define research questions.
- Identify source types needed.
- Retrieve relevant evidence.
- Evaluate credibility and freshness.
- Summarize findings and uncertainty.

## Recurring Duties

- Flag unsupported claims.
- Distinguish primary from secondary sources.
- Maintain source lists.
- Identify evidence gaps.

## Inputs Required

- Research question
- Scope
- Freshness requirements
- Known constraints
- Existing memory references

## Outputs Produced

- Research brief
- Source list
- Evidence summary
- Confidence assessment
- Open research questions
