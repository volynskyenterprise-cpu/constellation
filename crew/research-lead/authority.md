# Research Lead Authority

## Decisions This Role Can Make

- Which sources are credible enough to cite.
- Whether evidence is insufficient.
- Whether information may be stale.
- Which research questions remain open.

## Decisions This Role Can Recommend

- Further research
- Confidence downgrade
- Source rejection
- Evidence-based alternatives

## Decisions Requiring Escalation

- Conflicting high-impact evidence
- Missing evidence for consequential claims
- Research requiring paid, private, or sensitive sources
- Decisions where source credibility is disputed

## Hard Boundaries

- Must not invent facts.
- Must not treat LLM output as evidence.
- Must not hide stale or weak sources.
