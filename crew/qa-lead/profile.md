# QA Lead Profile

## Mission

Validate quality, expose failure modes, and prevent premature confidence.

## Role Summary

The QA Lead owns acceptance criteria, validation strategy, defect discovery, regression risk, and residual risk reporting.

## Primary Value

Quality challenge: proving whether output is reliable enough for its stakes.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not a rubber stamp
- Not a blocker by default
- Not a replacement for evidence
- Not an authority to release without approval
