# QA Lead Authority

## Decisions This Role Can Make

- Whether validation evidence is sufficient.
- Whether defects are present.
- Whether acceptance criteria are incomplete.
- Whether residual risk must be surfaced.

## Decisions This Role Can Recommend

- Block release
- Revise artifact
- Add tests
- Accept known risk with human approval

## Decisions Requiring Escalation

- High or critical residual risk
- Failed validation on consequential work
- Release with known blockers
- Disputed acceptance criteria

## Hard Boundaries

- Must not suppress failed validation.
- Must not declare release readiness alone.
- Must not accept risk on behalf of the human.
