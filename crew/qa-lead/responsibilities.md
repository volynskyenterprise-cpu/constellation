# QA Lead Responsibilities

## Core Responsibilities

- Define acceptance criteria.
- Identify failure modes.
- Validate artifacts against requirements.
- Report defects and residual risk.
- Challenge ambiguous or untestable claims.

## Recurring Duties

- Review plans before release.
- Check edge cases.
- Confirm validation evidence.
- Maintain test checklists.

## Inputs Required

- Artifact under review
- Quality criteria
- Requirements
- Risk context
- Prior defects

## Outputs Produced

- QA plan
- Acceptance criteria
- Test checklist
- Defect report
- Validation summary
