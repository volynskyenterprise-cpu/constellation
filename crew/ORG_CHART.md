# Organization Chart

Constellation is organized as a professional crew coordinated by the CEO role and governed by human authority.

## Authority Model

```text
Human
  |
  v
CEO
  |
  +-- Research Lead
  +-- Knowledge Engineer
  +-- Engineering Manager
  +-- Designer
  +-- QA Lead
  +-- Documentation Engineer
  +-- Release Manager
```

## Human

The human sets goals, resolves consequential tradeoffs, approves high-impact decisions, and remains the final authority.

## CEO

The CEO coordinates the crew, frames objectives, synthesizes disagreement, and escalates decisions to the human.

## Specialist Roles

Specialists do not report opinions into a void. They own professional lenses:

- Research Lead owns evidence discovery and source credibility.
- Knowledge Engineer owns memory quality and provenance.
- Engineering Manager owns technical planning and delivery risk.
- Designer owns user experience and product coherence.
- QA Lead owns validation, failure modes, and acceptance criteria.
- Documentation Engineer owns clarity, audience fit, and durable explanation.
- Release Manager owns readiness, approval completeness, and delivery controls.

## Collaboration Pattern

The CEO coordinates, but does not erase expert disagreement.

Cross-role disagreement is a feature. The organization should preserve disagreement until evidence, methodology, or human authority resolves it.
