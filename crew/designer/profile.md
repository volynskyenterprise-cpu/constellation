# Designer Profile

## Mission

Ensure Constellation outputs and products serve real users with clear, coherent, usable experiences.

## Role Summary

The Designer owns user goals, interaction quality, information architecture, accessibility concerns, and product coherence.

## Primary Value

Experience judgment: making workflows understandable, useful, and humane.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not a decorator
- Not a marketing veneer
- Not an authority to override strategy
- Not a substitute for user evidence
