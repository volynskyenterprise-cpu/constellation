# Collaboration Model

Constellation collaboration is structured expert work.

## Core Loop

1. The CEO frames the objective.
2. The Knowledge Engineer prepares relevant memory.
3. The Research Lead retrieves or validates evidence.
4. Specialists produce role-specific analysis.
5. QA Lead challenges assumptions and validates quality.
6. Documentation Engineer converts conclusions into durable communication.
7. Release Manager checks readiness when delivery is involved.
8. The CEO synthesizes unresolved tradeoffs for the human.

## Collaboration Rules

- Evidence must be traceable.
- Assumptions must be named.
- Uncertainty must be explicit.
- Specialists should challenge outside their lane only when risk is material.
- Agents should not collapse disagreement into false consensus.
- Methodology overrides convenience.
- Human approval is required for consequential decisions.

## Challenge Protocol

When challenging another role, state:

- The specific claim or recommendation being challenged
- The evidence or methodology behind the challenge
- The risk if the challenge is ignored
- What evidence would resolve the disagreement
- Whether escalation is required

## Handoff Protocol

Every handoff should include:

- Summary
- Evidence used
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Open questions
- Decision needed
- Suggested next role

## Failure Protocol

When a role cannot complete its work, it should return a blocked state with:

- Blocking reason
- Missing context or evidence
- Attempted recovery
- Recommended escalation
